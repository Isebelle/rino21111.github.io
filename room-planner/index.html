Lighting assumptions

The original plan’s top edge faces west; north is to the right. Sun direction follows the selected date and Harrison location (40.74642° N, 74.1571° W). Full walls and a ceiling cast shadows even in cutaway view; the apartment entry door is treated as closed. Interior doors are open. Clear sky and simplified diffuse light. Added lamps cast light and shadows; controls provide illustrative brightness and warmth, without a calibrated lux model. Window heights, ceiling, and materials are estimates. Nearby buildings, trees, balcony overhangs, and glass transmission are not modeled. Brightness is illustrative, not a lux prediction. Solar calculation reference.
